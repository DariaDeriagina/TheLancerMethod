How to Turn a Vague Idea Into a Bold Offer People Want to Buy

From “I have this idea…” to “It’s live, it’s selling, and it’s aligned.”

August 20, 2024

You don’t need a full business plan or 6 months of prep.

You need a process that brings the right idea to the surface, sharpens it, and makes it real.

Step 1: Extract the Essence

That idea? The one floating around in your head, your voice notes, or your Notes app?

Start by asking:

  • Who would this help?
  • What problem does it solve?
  • What transformation does it lead to?

💡 Your idea becomes stronger the clearer its outcome gets.

Step 2: Name the Value

People don’t buy ideas.
They buy outcomes.
Results.
Relief.

➡️ Turn “I help people get unstuck” into
“I help creatives turn their scattered notes into a signature offer they can sell.”

Step 3: Shape the Container

What form does this take?

  • A 1:1 session?
  • A group program?
  • A digital guide?
  • A retainer service?

Step 4: Give It Structure—and a Price

  • Session count, duration, milestones
  • Deliverables, tools, touchpoints
  • Price that reflects the transformation

And here’s a secret:
You don’t have to “be ready.” You just have to start.

“You don’t need another brainstorm. You need an offer people get — and want.”
  • Clarify the real idea (not just the one you think you “should” offer)
  • Build the right structure (custom to your life + style)
  • Launch it with clarity, confidence, and traction
