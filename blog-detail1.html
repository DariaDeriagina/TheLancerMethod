The 3 Invisible Blocks That Kill Great Ideas

From perfectionism to overwhelm—why you keep circling instead of building, and what to do instead.

September 5, 2024

The 3 most common blocks I see?

And how to outsmart them—so you stop spinning, and start building.

1. Perfectionism in Planner’s Clothing

You say you’re “just getting organized” or “waiting until it’s fully ready.” But deep down? You’re stalling. You’re so afraid of doing it wrong, you’d rather not do it yet at all.

🚫 The Truth: Progress doesn’t come from polish—it comes from starting.

✅ The Shift: Let the first version be messy. Done > perfect.

2. Overwhelm Masquerading as Multi-Passionate

You’ve got 12 tabs open and 8 notebooks full of notes. Every idea feels exciting. But none of them are moving.

🚫 The Truth: You’re not “multi-passionate.” You’re unprioritized.

✅ The Shift: Choose one thread. Build momentum there. Then expand.

3. “I’ll Know It When I See It” Syndrome

You’re waiting for a magical bolt of clarity to show up one day. But clarity doesn’t usually strike—it builds.

🚫 The Truth: Most people don’t get clarity and then act. They act—and then clarity comes.

✅ The Shift: Start building in one direction. The fog lifts as you move.

“You don’t need to fix yourself. You just need a method that gets you moving again.”

So how do you outsmart the blocks?
You stop trying to “think your way through.”
And you start moving—with a framework that guides and holds you.

  • Plan your path with structure and intention
  • Execute with real-time support and traction
